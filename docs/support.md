# Voices in View Support

For help with Voices in View, [open a support issue](https://github.com/zredlined/voices-in-view-ios/issues/new). Include your iPhone model and iOS version, but do not include private transcript text.

## Choosing an input

- **iPhone** uses the built-in microphone and requires no extra hardware.
- **USB** uses a compatible microphone or receiver connected to the iPhone.
- **AirPods** uses the microphone in the selected AirPods and requests Far Field capture when supported.

Tap **Test** before starting captions to confirm that the input meter responds to the person speaking.

## AirPods setup

1. Put on the AirPods you want to use and connect them to the iPhone.
2. In Voices in View, select **AirPods** under Input. If more than one pair is available, choose the pair you are wearing.
3. Tap **Test** and confirm that the AirPods name appears and the input meter moves.
4. If you rely on Transparency, Hearing Aid, or Conversation Boost, confirm it is enabled in Control Center and that you can still hear the room comfortably.
5. Start captions.

Voices in View cannot enable or change AirPods hearing features. If hearing the room becomes reduced or uncomfortable, stop the session and select the iPhone or USB input.

## DJI Mic Mini setup

1. Insert DJI's phone USB-C adapter into the receiver's expansion port.
2. Power on the receiver and transmitters.
3. Double-press the receiver's link button. A cyan status LED indicates Stereo mode; green indicates Mono or Safety Track.
4. Connect the receiver directly to the iPhone.
5. Open Voices in View, select **USB**, and verify that the input shows two channels.

The app reports audio level and clipping from the incoming sound. DJI transmitter battery and radio signal strength are only visible on the receiver itself.

## Troubleshooting

- If USB audio is missing, disconnect the receiver, make sure it is powered on, and reconnect it using the phone adapter.
- If the wrong AirPods are selected, tap the arrow beside **AirPods** under Input and choose the pair you are wearing.
- If AirPods do not appear, confirm they are connected in iPhone Settings, then return to Voices in View and tap **Test** again.
- If captions do not start, check microphone permission in Settings.
- The US English speech model may need to download before the first session. Once installed, live transcription works offline.
